Technical Approach

A practical IoT architecture connecting field sensing with monitoring, analytics and automated control.

01 • Sensing LayerFlow, water-level and quality sensors collect field data from tanks, pipelines and water systems.
02 • Controller LayerAn ESP32 or suitable microcontroller processes readings and controls connected devices.
03 • Communication LayerWi-Fi or another suitable IoT method transfers readings to the monitoring platform.
04 • Cloud & Data LayerSensor data is stored and converted into trends, records and system status.
05 • Intelligence LayerThresholds and usage patterns help identify abnormal flow, leakage and overflow risk.
06 • Control & Alert LayerUsers receive alerts and configured pumps or valves can respond automatically.
